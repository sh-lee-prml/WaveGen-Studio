# Contribute a voice

[한국어](VOICE_CONTRIBUTIONS_KO.md) · [Submit a voice](https://github.com/sh-lee-prml/WaveGen-Studio/issues/new?template=voice_submission.yml)

We welcome clear English reference recordings that other users can select in the voice library. Send a sample and its exact Reference Text, with the information below. Maintainers will review a contribution and may include it in a future release; submission is not automatic acceptance.

## What to provide

- **Audio:** preferably a clean, unprocessed mono WAV of one speaker saying a complete 3–10 second utterance; keep it under 30 seconds. Avoid music, other speakers, and cuts through words. Do not add artificial silence to reach a target length.
- **Reference Text:** the exact English words in the file. Include a transcript file or paste the text into the issue. A rough description is not a transcript.
- **Display name:** a short name or fictional alias. Do not imply another person's identity or endorsement. Optional artwork must also be yours to share and have its own license/source.
- **Source and modifications:** who recorded it, the original URL if applicable, and any resampling, trimming, gain adjustment, or enhancement already performed.
- **License and attribution:** the exact license name/text or permission grant, its source URL when available, required credit, and whether redistribution and derived processing are permitted. A free download is not itself permission to redistribute.
- **Speaker permission:** state whether you are the speaker; otherwise explain the permission to share this recording as a reusable TTS reference and create synthesized speech from it. A recording license and speaker permission may be different things.

GitHub issues and attachments are public. Do not upload private recordings, contact details, IDs, or signed personal documents. Summarize the permission and let the maintainer request a suitable verification method if needed. Do not submit a downloaded celebrity clip, another user's voice, or a recording whose use and redistribution you cannot authorize.

## How review works

We check whether the audio opens, the transcript matches, the reference is usable, and the source, license, credit, and permission are sufficiently clear. We may request a better clip or more information. A sample can be declined for quality, scope, or unclear permission. The app does not infer consent from the sound of a voice.

If accepted, the recording keeps its declared license and attribution. Preparation changes such as conversion to 24 kHz mono PCM will be recorded. Inclusion is a reusable reference profile, not a promise to train a new model, reproduce a person's identity perfectly, or endorse a speaker. Contributors can propose corrections through an issue.

The Studio code's MIT license does not automatically apply to submitted recordings. The included WaveGen models remain **CC BY-NC 4.0**, even when a reference recording has more permissive terms. See [Licensing](LICENSING.md).
